Events

ITC IITB hosts a variety of technical clubs that cater to a wide range of interests. These clubs provide students with opportunities to explore their passions, collaborate on projects, and develop their skills in a supportive environment.

From robotics and coding to aeromodelling and electronics, our clubs cover diverse technical domains. Each club organizes workshops, competitions, and projects that allow members to apply their knowledge and creativity. Joining a club is a great way to connect with like-minded peers and gain hands-on experience in your area of interest.
